Blood Relation – Reasoning (Beginner Friendly)

Blood Relation questions family ke relations par based hote hain — jaise father, mother, brother, sister, uncle, aunt etc. Exam ka kaam hota hai in relations ko confuse karna, aur tumhara kaam hota hai **diagram bana ke clarity lana**.

Why Blood Relation is Important?

  • Har banking exam me aata hi aata hai
  • Concept simple hai, questions tricky
  • Diagram bana = accuracy high
  • Time kam lagta hai agar practice ho

Basic Symbols (Must Remember)

♂ Male (Father, Son, Brother)
♀ Female (Mother, Daughter, Sister)
— Marriage
| Child connection

⚠ Symbol galat banaya = poora answer galat

Basic Relations (Foundation)

  • Father’s son = Brother
  • Mother’s brother = Maternal Uncle
  • Father’s sister = Paternal Aunt
  • Brother’s son = Nephew
  • Sister’s daughter = Niece

Real Life Connection

Family gatherings me relations samajhna jitna confusing hota hai, exam usi confusion ko paper me daal deta hai. Difference sirf itna hai — yahan logic lagta hai, emotions nahi.

Examples with Solutions

Q1. A is the brother of B. B is the mother of C.

How is A related to C?

Solution: Maternal Uncle

Q2. X is father of Y. Y is sister of Z.

Solution: X is father of Z

Q3. P is daughter of Q. Q is wife of R.

Solution: R is father of P

Q4. A’s son is B. B’s sister is C.

Solution: A is parent of C

Q5. If A is brother of B and B is brother of C.

Solution: A & C may be brothers or cousins (gender not fixed)

🔥 Short Tricks (Exam Gold)

  • Always draw family tree (never solve in head)
  • Mark gender immediately when given
  • “Only son / only daughter” words matter a lot
  • Brother–Sister chain = same generation
  • Wife/Husband relation = marriage line
  • Gender not given? Answer will be “cannot be determined”

Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

  • Assume gender without statement
  • Diagram skip karna (sabse badi galti)
  • Son–Brother confusion
  • Maternal / Paternal mix up
  • Overthinking simple relations
