Types of Automation Agents You Can Add
1. Communication Agents
WhatsApp Agent: Auto-reply, broadcast messages, collect files, create tasks from messages.
Email Agent: Auto-reply, forward important emails, tag or categorize emails, send newsletters.
SMS Agent: Send reminders, alerts, OTPs, or promotions automatically.
Social Media Agent: Auto-posting, comment monitoring, engagement tracking, alert for mentions.

2. CRM & Lead Management Agents
Lead Capture Agent: Capture leads from forms, WhatsApp, social media and enter into CRM automatically.
Lead Nurturing Agent: Send follow-up messages, schedule calls, or move leads through pipeline stages
Customer Feedback Agent: Send surveys or feedback requests after service delivery.

3. Project & Task Automation Agents
Task Creation Agent: Convert client emails, messages, or WhatsApp tasks into project tasks.
Deadline Reminder Agent: Notify teams and clients about upcoming deadlines automatically.
Progress Update Agent: Auto-send project status updates or generate reports.

4. AI & Analytics Agents
Chatbot Agent: Answer FAQs, provide product info, handle simple queries.
Sentiment Analysis Agent: Monitor social media or messages for positive/negative feedback.
Recommendation Agent: Suggest products, content, or next steps based on user behavior.

5. File & Document Agents
File Organizer Agent: Automatically sort files from WhatsApp, email, or drives.
Document Conversion Agent: Convert PDFs, images, or spreadsheets into required formats automatically.
Report Generation Agent: Auto-generate reports from collected data.

6. Integration Agents
Calendar Agent: Schedule meetings, send reminders, sync events across platforms.
Payment & Invoice Agent: Send invoices, reminders, and track payments automatically.
Third-Party API Agent: Connect with apps like Google Sheets, Slack, Trello, or e-commerce platforms to sync data.

7. Monitoring & Alert Agents
System Health Agent: Monitor uptime, performance, and errors.
Notification Agent: Alert clients or team about critical events, low stock, or missed tasks.
Analytics Alert Agent: Notify when key KPIs are met or drop below threshold.
